Part 1Why Have We Dropped?

Luckily for us! We’ve had no news, bad news (Blue Origin explosion/delay), AND skyrocketing interest rates.

I’m not going to get into the reasons why interest rates have risen (Trump), but they have.

It’s become increasingly clear that ASTS is part of a large index/basket of pre-revenue stocks that essentially trade in sync with each other based on macroeconomic variables.

Don’t believe me? Just look at $QBTS, $ASTS, $FLY, $KRKNF, and $RKLB over the past year.

Until ASTS starts generating actual operating revenue in the first half of 2027, you shouldn’t expect the stock to make any sense in terms of its direction or price movements. It’s going to continue being driven by macroeconomic conditions, speculation, and whatever news happens to come out.

Which is exactly why I keep telling everyone:

JUST BUY WEEKLY.
