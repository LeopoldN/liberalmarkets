Every market, at the close

Stocks, Treasury yields, currencies, commodities and crypto: how far each price has moved, where it stands against its record, and how it did in every year since its data begins.

The heatmap

Each square’s colour is its change over the period picked. Stocks and crypto are sized by approximate market value, sector funds by their approximate weight in the S&P 500; indexes, yields, currencies and commodities are drawn the same size. Pick a square to chart it below.

The long view

Year by year

Change in each calendar year

Each square is the change from the last close of one year to the last close of the next. The current year runs to the latest close. Yields show the change in basis points (100 bp is one percentage point).

Every price

Sources: Yahoo Finance for stocks, indexes, sector funds, futures and crypto; FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, for Treasury yields, exchange rates, Brent, WTI and Henry Hub gas, with Yahoo Finance filling the days FRED has yet to publish. Daily closes, updated each evening after the US close. Stock prices are adjusted for splits but not dividends, so returns leave out dividends. Gold, silver and copper are front-month futures; steel is the VanEck Steel ETF (SLX).
