      How to play

      1. The overview emphasizes leading export categories. Tile areas are fitted for readability; printed percentages are the true shares.
      2. Use the color bar to open any category, including categories omitted from the overview.
      3. Guess a country. A wrong guess shows the distance and direction to the answer.
      4. Use KM or MI in the header to choose your distance unit.
      5. Find the country in six guesses.
