    How to play

    Select two countries, then choose Match pair. Find all four pairs before making four mistakes.

    There is one pair for each rank: a country and its 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th largest trading partner. Each country is used once. Pairs can be solved in any order.

    Rankings are directional: one country’s top partner need not rank it first in return. The reveal shows which country the rank belongs to. All four pairs together have one solution.

    Trade means combined goods imports and exports, using the same 2024 data as Truddies. A new puzzle arrives daily at 00:00 UTC. Progress is saved in this browser.
